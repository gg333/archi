Features

Everything an archive app should do

macOS handles basic ZIPs. Archi is a complete archive manager: browse before you extract, build and edit archives, and keep every operation local, observable, and reversible.

Open and preview files

Browse, search, and test archives, then double-click a safe file to open it or press Spacebar for native Quick Look.

Safe, staged extraction

Output is validated in a private staging area first. Unsafe paths, links, collisions, and source replacement are blocked.

Create and safely edit

Create ZIP, 7z, TAR.GZ, TAR.XZ, TAR.ZST, GZIP, XZ, and Zstandard files. Add, delete, or rename entries in ZIP and 7z.

Fits into Finder

Open archives from Finder, and run five Finder Services — Extract Here, Extract to Folder, Test, Compress to ZIP, and Compress with Options.

Private by design

No accounts, uploads, analytics, advertising, or tracking. Passwords exist only for the active operation.

Cancellable, observable jobs

See progress, the current entry, speed, and warnings. Cancel while archive work is running; final output commits atomically.

Privacy & safety

Your archives never leave your Mac

Archi has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or usage tracking. It does not upload archive contents, file names, passwords, history, or diagnostics — archive operations don't even need a network connection.

Passwords stay private

Passwords are kept only for the active operation, never written to settings, history, process arguments, or diagnostics.

Extraction is staged & checked

Traversal paths, absolute paths, archive links, unsafe collisions, and source replacement are all blocked before install.

Nothing phones home

No crash-report uploads, no update pings, no telemetry of any kind in Archi 0.4.0.

You control local history

Up to ten recent archive paths stay on your Mac — disable or clear them any time from Settings.
