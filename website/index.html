Features

Everything an archive app should do

macOS handles basic ZIPs. Archi is a complete archive manager: browse before you extract, build and edit archives, and keep every operation local, observable, and reversible.

Look before you extract

Browse, search, sort, and test archives first — inspect sizes, ratios, and warnings before a single file lands on disk.

Encrypted ZIP & 7z

Create AES-256 encrypted archives, including 7z archives with encrypted file names. Open password-protected RAR and ZIP.

Edit archives in place

Add, delete, and rename entries inside ZIP and 7z archives, and read or edit UTF-8 ZIP comments — no repacking by hand.

Multi-volume archives

Create and extract split ZIP and 7z sets with configurable volume sizes, from 10 MiB parts up to multi-gigabyte spans.

Fits into Finder

Open archives from Finder, and run five Finder Services — Extract Here, Extract to Folder, Test, Compress to ZIP, and Compress with Options.

Safe, staged extraction

Output is validated in a private staging area first. Unsafe paths, links, collisions, and source replacement are blocked before install.

Thoughtful conflicts

When files collide, choose Ask, Replace, Skip, or Keep Both — and let Archi open the finished destination in Finder for you.

Cancellable jobs

Every operation shows progress, current entry, speed, and warnings — and can be cancelled cleanly at any point. Handles 100,000-entry archives.

Privacy & safety

Your archives never leave your Mac

Archi has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or usage tracking. It does not upload archive contents, file names, passwords, history, or diagnostics — archive operations don't even need a network connection.

Passwords stay private

Passwords are kept only for the active operation, never written to settings, history, process arguments, or diagnostics.

Extraction is staged & checked

Traversal paths, absolute paths, archive links, unsafe collisions, and source replacement are all blocked before install.

Nothing phones home

No crash-report uploads, no update pings, no telemetry of any kind in Archi 0.2.0.

You control local history

Up to ten recent archive paths stay on your Mac — disable or clear them any time from Settings.
